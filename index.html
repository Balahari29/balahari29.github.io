Project 01 · Brazilian e-commerce

Olist Customer Retention & Delivery Analysis

  • MySQL
  • Python
  • Excel
  • Power BI
View code on GitHub: Olist retention analysis

Problem

Does late delivery hurt reviews, and why do so few customers come back?

Data

Orders
99,441
Tables
9
Valid orders
97,905
Customers
94,703

Olist Brazilian e-commerce dataset, Kaggle.

Approach

  • MySQL star schema with 19 data-quality checks. They caught the trap that customer_id changes with every order, so repeat buyers must use customer_unique_id.
  • Mann-Whitney U test with a bootstrap confidence interval.
  • RFM segmentation and cohort retention.
  • Excel seller scorecard.
  • Power BI star schema and DAX measures designed, each with an SQL-computed expected value for testing.

Findings

  • −2.02 ★Late orders score 2.02 stars lower (2.27 vs 4.29; 95% CI 1.98–2.06; p < 0.001; large effect).
  • 74%of late-order reviews were written before the parcel arrived. Counting only reviews written after delivery, the gap is 0.76 stars.
  • 3.03%of customers bought twice.
  • 29.40%of revenue comes from “New – high value” customers, who are 15.56% of customers.
  • 11.64%of orders are late beyond 1,500 km, up from 4.49% under 100 km.
  • 21.46%late in AL and 17.53% in MA, vs 6.79% overall.

Recommendations

  1. Send the review survey only after delivery is confirmed, plus proactive delay alerts.
  2. Add delivery buffer on long routes. Fix the worst states and the top 20 sellers by excess late orders (457 excess late orders).
  3. Run a second-purchase campaign for New – high value customers, with a holdout group.
